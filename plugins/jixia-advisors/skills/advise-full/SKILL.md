# Advise, menu first

Run the shared `advise` skill in **inspect** mode on the user's question or draft: open [the advise skill](../advise/SKILL.md), follow its Inspect instructions, and stop at the menu until the user accepts or changes the selection. Everything after that (gating, lenses, the open deliberation, and the ruling) is the advise skill's.
