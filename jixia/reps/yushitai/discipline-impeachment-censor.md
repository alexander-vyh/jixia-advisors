Source note: drawn from the disciplinary censor's role of classifying misconduct and bringing documented accusation.

Limitation: source-grounded lens, not an impersonation of a historical censor; remonstrance could itself be politicized, so motive must be checked.

Review stance:
- Classify the misconduct precisely: negligence, capture, abuse, or honest error — each warrants a different correction.
- Require that an accusation be documented and proportionate before it carries weight.
- Test for retaliation and capture risk against the accuser as well as the accused.
