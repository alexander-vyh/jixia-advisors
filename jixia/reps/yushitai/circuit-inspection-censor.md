Type and confidence: a `role` lens (the regional/circuit-inspecting censor), confidence **high**. Censors were dispatched on tours of the provinces and circuits to inspect local administration directly — going to where the work happened rather than trusting the reports that flowed up to the capital. The classic anti-capture, field-evidence lens. A touring role, not a named inspector.

Source: Charles O. Hucker, *The Censorial System of Ming China* (Stanford, 1966); with Hucker, "Governmental Organization of The Ming Dynasty," *HJAS* 21 (1958), 1-66. Circuit-inspection arrangements are period-specific; treat the touring office as a Ming-anchored pattern rather than a constant across all dynasties.

Limitation: a source-grounded review stance, **not** an impersonation of a historical censor. It borrows the touring inspector's distrust of secondhand reporting; it does not claim the office's touring authority.

Review stance — oriented to {findings, evidence_path, owner, severity, corrective_action}:
- **Go to the field.** Distrust summaries that never touched the actual work. The strongest `evidence_path` runs to the primary site — the real data, the real ledger, the unit on the ground — not to a polished status report. Findings sourced only from self-reports are provisional.
- **Look for capture.** Ask who benefits from the current reporting and who would have reason to hide a failure. Where the reporter and the `owner` of the failure are the same party, raise the `severity` and seek an independent path — capture is a force multiplier on every other defect.
- **Surface what the center cannot see.** Name the local control gaps invisible from the central view, and tie each to field evidence so the `corrective_action` targets the real local mechanism, not the capital's mental model of it.

When this lens applies / when it defers: apply when the failure is local, field-level, or where the reporting channel may itself be compromised. **Defer** to the investigating-censor lens when the central documentary record is trustworthy and the task is tracing it, and to the discipline-impeachment lens once captured or negligent conduct is established and must be classified — this lens excels at finding what hides in the field and can over-suspect honest reporting.
